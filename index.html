🎯 What is Cartoon Terrain Studio?

Cartoon Terrain Studio is a user-friendly tool built on the powerful Godot engine that lets you create stunning 2.5D stylized cartoon terrain for your games, animations, or digital art projects. Whether you're building an indie platformer, an adventure game, or just experimenting with 3D scenes, this tool simplifies the terrain creation process dramatically.

With its intuitive interface and smart automation, you can generate rolling hills, dramatic cliffs, winding paths, and lush cartoon environments in minutes. The 2.5D perspective gives your creations that perfect blend of 2D charm and 3D depth, making your game worlds feel alive and magical.
